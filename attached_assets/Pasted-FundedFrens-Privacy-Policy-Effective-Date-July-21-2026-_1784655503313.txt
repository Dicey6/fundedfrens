FundedFrens Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026

1. Introduction

FundedFrens (“FundedFrens”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your information when you access or use the FundedFrens website, dashboard, Telegram trading bot, and any related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

By creating an account or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

⸻

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the Platform, we may collect the following categories of information.

Account Information

When you register an account, we may collect:

* Email address
* Username
* Encrypted password (managed through authentication services)
* Account creation date
* Account status

⸻

Profile Information

You may choose to provide:

* Payout wallet address
* Telegram username
* Referral code
* Profile preferences

⸻

Challenge Information

We collect information related to your Demo Challenge, including:

* Purchased challenge plan
* Challenge status
* Challenge start date
* Challenge completion date
* Trading days completed
* Performance statistics
* Risk metrics

⸻

Trading Analytics

Trading information generated by the FundedFrens Telegram Bot may include:

* Tokens traded
* Entry market capitalization
* Exit market capitalization
* Invested SOL
* Current value
* Profit and loss
* Profit percentages
* Position sizes
* Hold duration
* Stop Loss usage
* Take Profit usage
* Trailing Stop usage
* Auto Sell usage
* Portfolio performance

This information is used solely to power your analytics dashboard.

⸻

Payment Information

When purchasing a challenge, we may collect:

* Order details
* Transaction signature
* Payment wallet address
* Payment amount
* Payment status
* Blockchain confirmation data

FundedFrens does not collect or store your wallet’s private keys or recovery phrases.

⸻

Telegram Information

If you connect your Telegram account, we may collect:

* Telegram User ID
* Telegram username
* Link status
* Link date
* Connection token

This information is used only to securely connect your Telegram account with your FundedFrens account.

⸻

Device Information

We may automatically collect:

* Browser type
* Device type
* Operating system
* IP address
* Language settings
* Time zone
* Log information
* Session information

This information helps improve security and platform performance.

⸻

FundedFrens may access publicly available blockchain information for purposes including:

FundedFrens cannot modify or remove blockchain records.
